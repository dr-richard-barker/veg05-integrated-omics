# DESeq2 and WGCNA re-run (2026-10-14)

The `/abai check` of 13–14 Oct found two analysis errors in the RNA-seq steps:

| Script | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `03_rnaseq_deseq2.R` | `flight_vs_ground` was the flight coefficient of the `~ flight * light` model, i.e. the flight effect under Red (the reference light); `red_vs_blue` was the light effect in Ground plants only | each main effect is averaged over both levels of the other factor (flight coefficient + ½ interaction; −light coefficient − ½ interaction), shrunk with ashr (apeglm cannot shrink a combination of coefficients) |
| `06_wgcna.R` | plants matched to dysbiosis samples by substring, so flight plant SN01 also matched SN010; every p-value used n = 21 (leaf) or 15 (root), although the dysbiosis indices are missing for some plants | plants matched by number, exactly; each p-value uses the number of plants that have that trait, written to new `n_*` columns |

The tables, Table S2, Table S5, Fig 3, Fig 4, the directionality heatmaps and the
manuscript text were regenerated from this re-run. This file records how.

## Controls: the unchanged scripts

- `03` reproduces `degs_summary.tsv` byte for byte. In `degs_Leaf_flight_vs_ground.tsv` and
  `degs_Leaf_red_vs_blue.tsv` every gene and every DEG call is the same; values differ by at
  most 0.06% (relative).
- `06` reproduces the soft-thresholding powers (20, 18), the modules and the hub genes
  exactly, and the flight and light correlations exactly.
- `06` does **not** reproduce the tracked dysbiosis correlations, except adventitious-root
  ITS. The tracked values are reproduced to within 1e-14 only if the ground samples whose
  microbiome ID writes the plant as `SN-0x` are left out: an earlier version of the matcher
  did not remove the hyphen. So the tracked `module_trait_correlations.tsv` used 9 of 21 leaf
  plants for 16S (flight only), 7 of 18 for leaf ITS, 10 of 15 for root 16S and 13 for root
  ITS (12 plants plus SN01 given SN010's value).
- Table S5 and `integration/networks/module_directionality.tsv` match the committed `06`
  (the control run) to within 1e-14, not the tracked correlation tables. Fig 4 had been drawn
  from the tracked tables.

Checks on the new contrasts: the fold changes equal the coefficient combinations to 1e-15, and
agree with the average of the per-light log ratios of group-mean normalised counts (genes with
every group mean > 100) at r = 0.9995 (leaf flight), 0.9989 (leaf light), 0.9994 and 0.9991
(root). The old flight term agrees with that average at r = 0.56 in leaf and 0.92 in root.

Four root genes absent from every ground sample have shrunk |log2FC| > 10 (up to 27); the old
flight term had three such genes.

`degs_Adv-Root_flight_vs_ground.tsv` is now tracked because Fig 3C is drawn from it.

  and SN010 has no leaf ITS sample, but the rule should be replaced when `07` is re-run.
