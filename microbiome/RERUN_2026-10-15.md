# Microbiome re-run from the OSD-766 reads (2026-10-15)

The `/abai check` of 13–14 Oct found problems in the microbiome steps that could only be fixed
by re-running them from the raw reads. Every microbiome table, the MOFA+ and module–taxon
results, the WGCNA dysbiosis correlations, Tables S3–S9 and the affected figures were
regenerated from this re-run. This file records how, and what changed.

## Fixes

| Script | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `01_parse_metadata.py` | OSD-766 labels the adventitious-root samples of ground plants SN02, SN04 and SN05 blue-rich, but labels those plants' leaf samples red-rich (as for every ground plant SN01–SN06), and OSD-767 names their root RNA-seq samples Red | the three samples (six files: 16S and ITS) are treated as red-rich, with a comment citing the conflicting records |
| `02_microbiome_dada2.R` | 16S was not run: the script loaded a saved result, and `process_16S()` stopped when building the phyloseq object, because it read the sequences back after renaming them `ASV001`, … | sequences are captured before renaming, as the ITS function already did; 16S is run |
| `02_microbiome_dada2.R` | 15 ITS samples have no reads after filtering, so `cbind()` recycled the shorter denoised/merged columns: 128 of 141 rows of `track_stats_ITS.tsv` were misaligned | read counts are matched to samples by name |
| `02_microbiome_dada2.R` | `assignTaxonomy()` bootstraps at random and no seed was set, so re-runs gave different labels for ASVs near the 50% cut-off | `set.seed(42)` before each call (tested: same seed identical, another seed changes 43 of 240 bootstrap values) |
| `04_community_health.R` | `merge()` re-sorts the alpha-diversity rows by sample, and the read depths were then attached in phyloseq order; 15 16S and 11 ITS rows had more ASVs than reads | depths are looked up by sample name |
| `04_community_health.R` | PERMANOVA reported only the whole model (fixed in the code in Phase 1, not re-run until now) | re-run with `by = "terms"`: flight, light, flight × light, added in that order |
| `05_functional_prediction.R`, `faprotax_collapse.py` | the custom FAPROTAX parser read three taxon lines of the database as group names (`Roseospira*mediosalina*`, `Methylobacterium*mesophilicum*`, `Bacteroides*thetaiotaomicron*`), matched taxon names as substrings and skipped the group set operations; the committed `05` also read the unfiltered table, in which FAPROTAX files SILVA's mitochondria (Rickettsiales) as intracellular parasites | FAPROTAX 1.2.12's own `collapse_table.py` on the 348 bacterial ASVs, with hyphens as word separators (see below); `faprotax_collapse.py` removed |
| `07_integration_mofa.R` | the microbiome samples were matched without regard to tissue (the sample with most reads won, usually root), and `prune_samples()` returned them in a different order from the names then assigned to the columns | only leaf samples are matched; columns are selected by sample ID before renaming; ASVs with no reads in those samples are dropped (they became all-missing features after scaling) |
| `07_integration_mofa.R` | dysbiosis looked up by substring plant ID (SN01 also matched SN010) | the indices of the same leaf samples used in the 16S and ITS views |

`08_correlation_networks.R` (fixed in Phase 1), `06_wgcna.R` (fixed in Phase 2) and
`09_directionality.R` Parts 2–3 were re-run on the new outputs without further changes.

## Inputs

- **Reads:** 566 FASTQ files (284 16S, 282 ITS; 1,346,771,904 bytes) from NASA OSDR OSD-766.
  All 566 match the MD5 checksums OSDR publishes (`GLDS-672_Amplicon_raw_md5sum_GLAmpSeq-{16S,ITS}.txt`).
- **Metadata:** `OSD-766_metadata_OSD-766-ISA.zip` (OSDR); sample metadata parsed from the
  file names by `01_parse_metadata.py`, as before.
- **SILVA 138.1** for DADA2 (Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4587955):
  `silva_nr99_v138.1_train_set.fa.gz` (MD5 `6b41db7139834c71171f8ce5b5918fc6`) and
  `silva_species_assignment_v138.1.fa.gz` (MD5 `f21c2d97c79ff07c17949a9622371a4c`), both
  matching the record.
- **UNITE:** the original run used a 10 Oct 2017 build that UNITE no longer distributes. The
  re-run uses the UNITE QIIME release of 1 Dec 2017 (https://doi.org/10.15156/BIO/587481), 99%
  set, converted as `02` describes (taxonomy prefixes removed). This changes a few ITS labels
  (below).
- **FAPROTAX 1.2.12** (Louca lab archive). Its licence requires that redistributed copies keep
  its notice, so it is not committed; `05` expects it in `data/microbiome/ref_db/FAPROTAX_1.2.12/`.

R 4.6.1; dada2 1.40.0, phyloseq 1.56.0, vegan 2.7-6, ALDEx2 1.44.0, MOFA2 1.22.1, WGCNA 1.74;
Python 3.9.6 with cutadapt 5.2 and mofapy2 0.7.5 (the version MOFA2 1.22.1 expects).

## Controls: the original scripts on the re-run reads

- **DADA2, 16S:** reproduces the tracked run exactly: the same 544 ASVs, and every column of
  `track_stats_16S.tsv` for all 142 samples (3,558,814 non-chimeric reads). 348 ASVs remain after
  removing 196 chloroplast and mitochondrial ASVs. With the seed, 6 of 544 genus labels differ
  from the tracked table; an unseeded run differed at 7 labels (6 genus, 1 phylum). All are
  bootstrap-borderline.
- **DADA2, ITS:** primer removal, filtering and reverse denoising reproduce exactly for all 141
  samples; forward denoising does not (81 of 141 samples equal), giving 78 ASVs and 156,102 reads
  against 77 and 160,316. Per sample, non-chimeric reads differ by a median of 3%. The likely
  cause is the dada2 version (1.34.0 originally). ASV order and labels are mostly kept; ASV009
  and ASV010 swap, and with the newer UNITE file ASV006 is *Fusarium* without a species call.
- **`04` (version on `main`):** for 16S reproduces alpha diversity (including the misplaced
  depths), dysbiosis, PERMANOVA and ALDEx2 exactly. For ITS the values are close (leaf model
  R² 0.456 vs 0.45, p 0.053 vs 0.054; leaf flight dysbiosis 3.69 vs 3.64).
- **`08`:** the significant module–taxon set is identical to the tracked one (29 pairs) and all
  2,207 16S correlations are identical.
- **`07`:** reproduces the tracked model closely (Factor 1: 48.0% of transcriptome variance,
  flight ρ = −0.76, padj 0.001) — on the mismatched samples.
- **FAPROTAX:** the original parser on the bacterial table, with species-level `*`-separated
  taxonomy and FAPROTAX 1.2.10, reproduces 23 of the 29 tracked groups exactly, including the
  spurious `Roseospira*mediosalina*` (31 ASVs). The tracked `cyanobacteria` group was split in
  FAPROTAX 1.2.4 (2020), so the original used an earlier release.
- **Figures:** `10`/`11` on the control outputs give the tracked Figs 2, 5, 7 and S2 (same
  text and drawn elements); the other differences are accounted for by the fixes and the ITS
  re-run.

## What changed

### Community composition (Table S4)

| | Flight | Light | Flight × light |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16S leaf (n = 21) | R² 0.28, p 0.001 | 0.03, p 0.51 | 0.11, p 0.035 |
| 16S root (n = 15) | 0.29, p 0.001 | 0.07, p 0.22 | 0.11, p 0.027 |
| ITS leaf (n = 18) | 0.36, p 0.007 | 0.03, p 0.41 | 0.06, p 0.15 |
| ITS root (n = 12) | 0.40, p 0.001 | 0.19, p 0.005 | 0.16, p 0.006 |

The leaf fungal community changes with flight (the tracked whole-model test gave p = 0.054).
Dysbiosis: 16S unchanged; ITS leaf flight 3.69 ± 2.78 vs ground 1.00 ± 0.22.

### Differential abundance (ALDEx2)

- 16S results are identical. ASV024, reported as *Pantoea*, has 49% genus bootstrap support with
  the seeded run and is labelled at family level (Erwiniaceae).
- ITS ASV006 (*Fusarium*) in roots: effect +1.91, BH-adjusted Welch p 0.053 (tracked 0.046); no
  longer passes. Its species call (*F. solani*) has 48% support.
- With the relabelled ground roots, ASV091 drops out of the 16S root light contrast.

### FAPROTAX (Table S6, Fig 7)

FAPROTAX matches whole words, and by default counts `-` as part of a word, so SILVA's merged
genus names match nothing (20 ASVs: 14 *Methylobacterium-Methylorubrum*, 4
*Burkholderia-Caballeronia-Paraburkholderia*, 1 *Allorhizobium-Neorhizobium-Pararhizobium-Rhizobium*,
1 *Pedococcus-Phycicoccus*). `05` runs it with `--valid_word_symbols ''`, so each genus in such a
name is matched (chosen on 15 Oct).

205 of 348 bacterial ASVs are assigned, to 44 groups. Largest: chemoheterotrophy (786,147
reads, 180 ASVs), aerobic chemoheterotrophy (688,214; 150), ureolysis (235,475; 31), nitrogen
fixation (223,554; 15), methylotrophy (96,868; 16), methanol oxidation (96,687; 15, exactly the
tracked figure; 14 of the 15 are *Methylobacterium-Methylorubrum*). Methanotrophy (181 reads)
and dark hydrogen oxidation (354 reads) are small; the tracked 96,514 and 103,694 reads came from
substring matching. With FAPROTAX's default 185 ASVs would be assigned, and the groups above would
lose the merged-name genera (methanol oxidation, for example, would have 354 reads).

### MOFA+ (Table S8, Figs 5 and S6)

18 leaf samples have RNA-seq, 16S and ITS from the same leaf; the views hold 2,000 genes, 101
16S ASVs and 40 ITS ASVs. Factor 1 explains 44.4% of transcriptome, 9.0% of 16S and 7.0% of ITS
variance, and correlates with flight (ρ −0.68, padj 0.037) and light (ρ +0.63, padj 0.053). Only
one flight blue-light leaf has ITS data, so flight and light are partly confounded here. No
other factor–trait correlation has padj < 0.05 (Factor 3 with 16S dysbiosis: ρ −0.61, padj
0.053). MOFA+ warns that 5 factors are many for 18 samples and that Factor 1 tracks the mean
level of one view.

### Module–taxon networks (Table S9, Figs 6 and S5)

Leaf results are unchanged (28 significant 16S pairs). With the relabelled ground roots, 15
(16S) and 12 (ITS) root plants match their RNA-seq samples, against 12 and 9 before, and root
ITS gives 3 significant pairs instead of 1: *Fusarium* ASV006 with the blue (ρ −0.93) and
turquoise (ρ +0.91) modules, and ASV034 with lightcyan (ρ −0.88). 31 pairs in all.

### WGCNA traits and directionality (Table S5, Fig 4)

16S dysbiosis correlations are unchanged; ITS ones move by at most 0.005, and the significant
modules are the same. The black module: r = −0.85, padj 0.009 (12 plants); within flight plants
r = −0.87 and within ground plants r = −0.75 (6 each). The ITS dysbiosis index's own correlation
with flight is now 0.65 in leaves and 0.71 in roots (0.64 and 0.70 in Phase 2). Directionality
classes are unchanged from Phase 2.

## Caveats

- Two flight adventitious-root ITS samples (SN03 and SN04, red-rich) kept one read each. They
  enter the dysbiosis indices, PERMANOVA and the WGCNA traits. Without them the black module's
  ITS correlation is r = −0.82 (p = 0.003, n = 10).
- PERMANOVA terms are sequential (flight first); in these unbalanced designs the light and
  interaction R² depend on that order.
- `07` labels its microbiome transform "CLR", but it centres each ASV across samples before
  scaling; the manuscript does not call it CLR.
